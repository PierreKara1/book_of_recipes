# Andalouse
Andalouse sauce is a popular Belgian and French cold sauce typically made with a mayonnaise base, tomato paste, peppers, and seasonings, often served with fries and fried foods. Read more at the Wikipedia.

## Ingredients

1 cup mayonnaise

2 tbsp tomato paste

2 tbsp finely diced red bell pepper

1 tbsp diced pimientos or roasted red peppers

1 tsp lemon juice or white wine vinegar

Pinch of cayenne pepper or paprika

Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

finely dice the red bell pepper and pimientos

combine the mayonnaise and tomato paste in a bowl and mix until smooth

stir in the diced peppers and lemon juice or vinegar

season with cayenne pepper, salt, and black pepper to taste

mix well and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving
