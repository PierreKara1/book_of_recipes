# Recipe for andalouse dauce

- Mayonnaise: 1 cup (base)
- Tomato paste: 2 tablespoons
- Bell pepper: 2 tablespoons finely diced red bell pepper
- Pimientos: 1 tablespoon diced pimientos (or roasted red peppers)
- Lemon juice: 1 teaspoon (or white wine vinegar)
- Pinch of cayenne pepper or paprika for mild heat and color
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper: To taste
- Optional: A pinch of sugar or finely minced shallot/onion for extra depth
